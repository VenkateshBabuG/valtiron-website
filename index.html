ABOUT VALTIRON

American project support.

VALTIRON is a US-based commercial door distribution business focused on helping construction customers source hollow metal doors, frames and related products.

Our product offering is informed by the catalogue of iCLEAN Hollow Metal Systems Pvt. Ltd. (IHMS), an India-based manufacturer. Product selection, pricing, lead times and documentation are handled on a project-by-project basis.
