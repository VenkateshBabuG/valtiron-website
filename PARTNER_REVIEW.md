## Decisions needed before commercial launch
1. Approve the proposed logo, or request an icon-only and transparent-background version for header/favicon. The artwork is a concept, not a registered trademark claim.
2. Confirm legal company name/spelling in Wyoming records (registration may read “Distrubution”). The website intentionally uses VALTIRON as the public brand without asserting a legal spelling.
3. Verify permission to publish manufacturer photos, specifications and brand references.
4. Confirm exact products, assembly certifications, lead times and US import/compliance documentation.
5. Set up a verified company email and working form backend before treating the RFQ form as live.
6. Review prices, delivery terms, warranty, returns and privacy policy before taking orders.
